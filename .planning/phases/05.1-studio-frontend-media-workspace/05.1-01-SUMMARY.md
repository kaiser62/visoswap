## Accomplishments
- Per-frame source re-embed tax removed: `Engine._source_embedding_store` serves a private memo keyed by realpath+mtime_ns+size (stat every call, cleared in `Engine.load`), taking mean swap latency from 147.65 ms to ~96–98 ms at 1080p.
- `tools/benchmark_engine.py --gate --idle-gpu` certifies the real cache against the embed_once control in one process and refuses to print a verdict from a contended card or an unacknowledged invocation.
- `docs/benchmark-baseline.md` § "Post-cache measurement (Phase 05.1 plan 01)" records the real number over the projection — projection reached (p50 byte-identical at 94 ms across all windows) — plus the settled D-14 finding: thread count within noise at 1 vs 8, `nThreadsSlider` stays exposed at fixture default, never advertised as a speedup.
- Stop→change face→start proven pure end-to-end on one project through the FastAPI TestClient with a marker-byte generator stub: no first-face frame files, no pre-stop frame rows, no first-run recorder output survive; all second-run frames carry the second face; a freshly built generator reports the second `source_face_path`; minimum frame priority lands at the timestamp closest to and not beyond the playhead.

## Ordering note (min-priority-near-playhead)

The purity test also pins the scheduler's claim-ordering behaviour: after a start with the
playhead partway into the clip, the first frames planned are those nearest the playhead —
read from `GET /api/projects/{id}/frames`, the minimum priority column value sits at the
timestamp closest to and not beyond the playhead. This matters downstream because
generation (~10 fps) runs behind playback (23.976 fps): nearest-previous overlay is the
design, not a fallback (docs/benchmark-baseline.md D-06 verdict).

## Decisions Made
- Cache key = `os.path.realpath` + `st_mtime_ns` + `st_size`, stat on every call; stat errors propagate rather than degrading to a path-only key (threat T-05.1-01-01).
- Memo cleared inside `Engine.load`'s existing rebind block so an embedding computed under one parameter tier can never serve another project (T-05.1-01-02).
- Gate pass condition: both real-run means within ±15% of control AND two-run spread ≤25%; missing `--idle-gpu` exits non-zero with reproduce instructions (T-05.1-01-06).
- Keep-on-stop for scheduler stop-time frame policy (user decision, recorded above).
