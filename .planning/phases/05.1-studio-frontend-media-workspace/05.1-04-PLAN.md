<objective>
Turn the settings app into the Studio shell without losing a single control.

Purpose: the product today is a settings editor with a header — a white, single-column
page whose whole body is the schema. The phase's user-facing surfaces (player, faces,
transport, results, jobs, gallery) have nowhere to live until the page becomes a card
layout, and the card layout is worthless in the wrong palette: the reference the user
built and approved is dark. This plan does the container and the colour, and nothing
else, so that every plan after it adds a card instead of arguing about the frame.

The hard part is not the layout. It is that the schema surface is load-bearing and
already gated: 201 controls must stay rendered and typed exactly as they are, through a
rewrite of the component that contains them.

Output: a dark token vocabulary defined once, primitives and controls that read from it,
and a two-column Studio layout whose Controls card holds today's sidebar and groups
untouched.
</objective>

    In `frontend/src/index.css`, extend the existing `@theme` block with the webui2 palette as
    Tailwind v4 colour tokens: `--color-bg`, `--color-card`, `--color-raised`, `--color-line`,
    `--color-text`, `--color-muted`, `--color-accent`, `--color-active`, `--color-good` and
    `--color-bad`, each set to the corresponding value read from webui2's `:root`. Naming them
    under the `--color-` namespace is what makes Tailwind generate `bg-card`, `border-line`,

    site would need an arbitrary-value bracket.

    foreground from the tokens on `body`. `color-scheme` is not cosmetic here: the schema
    surface is 201 native form controls — checkboxes, ranges, selects and their dropdown
    popups — and the parts of them the browser paints itself ignore Tailwind classes entirely.
    Without it the app is dark chrome full of white widgets.

    In `frontend/src/components/ui.tsx`, repoint the two exported colour constants at the
    accent and bad token values, keeping both export names and the trailing re-export line
    exactly as they are so no call site changes.

    Retarget every primitive to the tokens, preserving each component's props, variants,
    structure, ARIA attributes and the `role="dialog"` / `aria-modal` / `aria-label` triple on
    `Modal`. The mapping is fixed: card and modal surfaces take `bg-card`, inset or hovered
    surfaces take `bg-raised`, every hairline takes `border-line`, primary copy takes
    `text-text`, secondary and placeholder copy takes `text-muted`, the primary button and
    badge fill take `bg-accent` with a dark foreground, the destructive button fill takes
    `bg-bad`, the focus ring takes the accent token, and the skeleton and spinner track take
    `bg-raised` / `border-line` with the accent as the spinner's leading edge. The ghost button
    keeps a transparent fill and takes `text-muted` with a `bg-raised` hover.

    Change nothing else in this file. These primitives are rendered by tests that query by
    role and accessible name, and by a discard dialog whose accessible name is asserted
    verbatim.
